Skin Receipts Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Skin Receipts does not collect your data. There is no Skin Receipts account, no Skin Receipts server, and no analytics. Everything is stored on your device. If you switch on iCloud sync, your data moves between your own devices through your own iCloud account — it is never sent to us, and we cannot read it.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter stays in Skin Receipts' private storage area on your device:

  • Your quiz answers (skin type, hair type, concerns, preferences)
  • Your generated routine
  • Your daily check-offs, symptom logs and notes
  • Products you add to your shelf
  • Progress photos

With iCloud sync switched off — which is how the app ships — this information never leaves your device. If you delete the app, it is removed with it.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

You can turn on iCloud sync in Settings to keep your routine and logs consistent across your own devices. When you do:

  • Your data is stored in the private database of your personal iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit. It is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • We have no access to it. A CloudKit private database is readable only by you — not by the developer of the app, and not by anyone the developer could grant access to.
  • There is still no Skin Receipts server and no Skin Receipts account.
  • Progress photos are a separate switch, also off by default. Sync your routine without syncing your photos if you prefer.
  • Turning sync off returns the app to storing everything only on your device.

Progress photos

Photos you add are saved as files inside the app's private container with iOS file protection enabled. They are not shared and not readable by other apps. Choosing a photo uses the system picker, which gives Skin Receipts access only to the images you pick.

Photos are only ever copied off the device if you switch on both iCloud sync and the separate "include progress photos" option, in which case they go to your own iCloud private database and nowhere else.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS on your device. Skin Receipts does not use push notifications, has no push server, and does not receive a device token.

Third parties

Skin Receipts includes no third-party SDKs, no advertising, no trackers and no crash-reporting service. Nothing about your use of the app is shared with anyone. The only Apple services it can use are CloudKit, if you switch sync on, and local notifications, if you enable reminders.

Links out of the app

Citations in the ingredient library link to public sources such as PubMed, doi.org and regulatory websites. Opening one hands the link to your browser, and that site's own privacy policy applies from that point. Skin Receipts does not attach any identifier to those links.

Exporting and deleting

Settings → Export my data writes a JSON copy of your data and hands it to the iOS share sheet. That is the only route by which your data leaves the device, and you choose where it goes. Settings → Delete everything permanently removes your profile, routine, logs and every progress photo from the device.

Children

Skin Receipts is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Medical disclaimer

Skin Receipts is a tracking and education tool. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose conditions, and it does not prescribe treatment. Consult a dermatologist or your doctor for diagnosis and for prescription products.

Payments

Skin Receipts is currently free in full. If a paid tier is introduced, purchases will be handled entirely by Apple through the App Store — Skin Receipts never sees or stores your payment details.

Changes

If a future version changes what the app stores or how it transmits anything, this policy will be updated before that version ships and the change will be described in the release notes.
